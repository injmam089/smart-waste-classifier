        About Smart Waste Classifier

        A client-side Progressive Web App promoting sustainable waste sorting using client-side machine learning and actionable municipal guidance.

        🔬 Visual Feature Analysis Notice

        This app inspects optical visual features (color, contours, shape, and textures) with an image model. It does not measure physical weight or material purity.

        🔒 Privacy & Data Handling

        Camera frames are analyzed strictly on your local device. Images are never uploaded to any server. Scan logs are stored only in your browser's LocalStorage.
